# BNJC Lamp Run: shareable gameplay preview

A play-testing preview of Lamp Run, a short Chanukah arcade adventure from BNJC in Brighton & Hove to the Temple in Jerusalem. It is a single page that runs in a current browser, on desktop or on a phone held sideways. It is a prototype, not the live BNJC competition.

## The opening

Enter a name and email to start. This preview does not send or store the email, and nothing in it enters anyone into a prize draw. The name and your scores stay on your own device so you can try the flow. A shared leaderboard and any prize entry would need a secure online connection and approved competition terms.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Left / Right, or A / D | Move |
| Space | Jump (press again in the air for a super jump) |
| X, or click / tap the stage | Throw a latke toward that side |
| C | Switch weapon |
| Z | Sprint |
| Shift | Dash |
| Up / Down | Climb a ladder / drop a level |
| E | Go through a BNJC door (bank oil, refill energy, save your spot), take the lifeguard's shield, light the chanukiah |
| Esc | Pause |

On a phone, hold it sideways. The controls float over the bottom corners: left thumb for climb, left and right; right thumb for jump, throw, bank/use, sprint, dash and weapon. If you turn the phone upright, the game pauses and asks you to turn it back.

## The ending

After the Temple Snuffer falls, walk to the chanukiah. Stand under the raised shamash in the middle and press E, then stand under each branch and press E. Each light spends one banked oil drop. Cold gusts can put a branch out: press E under it again to relight it for free. If the oil runs out, the miracle lights the rest.

## Your runner

After your name and email, pick your runner: skin tone, hair colour, jacket and trousers colours, a head covering (none, kippah, beanie, cap or headscarf) and a scarf, or press "Surprise me". Your choice is remembered on your device.

## BNJC doors: bank, rest and save your spot

Oil you carry can be lost if you are hurt. About every other scene there is a navy BNJC door. Walk to it and press E (USE on a phone, or up). Inside, your oil pours into a jar and locks away (banked oil is safe and is what lights the chanukiah), your energy refills, some doors give a heart, and your spot is saved: if you are caught you restart at the last door you used.

## Power-ups

A sufganiyah is an oil magnet for 12 seconds, gelt doubles oil points for 12 seconds, and a gull feather gives higher jumps for 12 seconds. Wraps give a short speed boost, the aura is a 10-second shield and the coffee makes you bigger.

## Yofi coffee, oil shots and the lifeguard

Drink a Yofi coffee to grow to 1.5 times your size, with a bigger body to match; the next hit shrinks you back instead of costing a heart. A latke that touches a loose oil drop collects it for double. Stand by the promenade lifeguard and press E for a 10-second life-ring shield.

## Your nickname

The runner picker has a nickname box (press Auto for a friendly one). The scoreboard shows the nickname, not your name.

## The tutorial

Wick, a little flame, teaches the first two scenes. A speech bubble shows one step at a time with a pointer at what to do: walk, jump the bollard, grab oil, stun the car, jump on it, grab a wrap, bank oil, beat a sliding Yofi table, sprint and climb the ladder. Wick cheers every time you get one. On a phone the matching button pulses. On an iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, to play full screen without Safari's bars.

## Scene goals

Each scene has one goal, shown in the panel at the top left with your progress and its points: beat a baddie in the courtyard, collect all five wraps at Yofi, light four lanterns in the Lanes, a flawless tea party in the Pavilion Gardens (no hits), dodge three gull attacks on the promenade, gather 30 oil in the underpass, rest in the guest-room bed, bank your oil at the second Yofi, beat the Sea Life Shadow, beat the Greek war elephant on the road east (jump on its head, or throw latkes when it opens), and climb the i360 to its top platform. The baddies wear Greek helmets because the Chanukah story is about the Greek empire.

## Hidden surprises

There are a few small secrets in the run. Nothing in them is needed to finish, and they only add a little fun. Wick is worth a tap, and so is a nickname with a bit of history in it.

## Scoring

Oil drops are 10 points each, with a small bonus for streaks. Defeating baddies (a latke, or a jump-stomp after they open), dodging attacks, clean jumps over furniture, clearing chapters and lighting each chanukiah branch also score. Oil you bank is kept safe and is worth points at the end. The leaderboard in this preview is local to one browser on one device.

## Limits

- No shared online leaderboard, email submission or prize registration.
- Some foreground art and the balance of each scene still need play-testing across devices and browsers.
- Tested so far in a desktop Chrome browser with touch emulation; not yet on real phones, in Safari or in Firefox. Sound has not been checked.
- A run has a nine-minute ceiling.
